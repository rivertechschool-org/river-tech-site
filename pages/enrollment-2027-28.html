2027–28 Enrollment

River Tech School · One-time enrollment fees per household, separate from tuition

A head start for returning families

We’d love to see your family back next year. Interest in River Tech is growing quickly, so we open enrollment to current families first. We encourage you to enroll early for the best chance at the schedule you want.

Places are subject to availability for all students, including those returning. Once you’ve completed enrollment and paid the fee, look for a confirmation from River Tech that your child has a spot.
