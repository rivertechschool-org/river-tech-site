2027–28 Enrollment

River Tech School · One-time enrollment fees per household, separate from tuition

Planning to return next year? Please enroll early.

We would love to welcome your family back! Your child’s spot does not automatically carry over to next year. Completing enrollment tells us you intend to return.

Current families get the first opportunity to enroll. As each new window opens, more families can apply for the remaining seats.

Re-enroll for 2027–28 when your family’s enrollment window opens.

Enrollment opensWho can enroll
November 20Current full-time families
December 5Current part-time families, including those choosing full-time
January 1Families currently in our wait pool
January 21The public, where space remains

Demand is high, and space is limited. We strongly encourage you to enroll as soon as your window opens.

For example, a current full-time family waiting until mid-January would be applying after enrollment has also opened to current part-time families and our wait pool. By then, their child’s group could already be full.

Please complete enrollment and pay the household fee promptly. Your child’s place is secure once you receive a seat confirmation from River Tech. This applies to returning students as well as new students.
