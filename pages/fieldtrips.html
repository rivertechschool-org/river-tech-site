Trip overview

Dear parents,

Next week, River Tech students have two exciting field trips:

  • Wednesday, September 16 — Hidden Acres Farm and Student Farmers Market
  • Thursday, September 17 — NIC Robotics Department Tour for full-time high school students

One of our favorite annual field trips is back! Students will visit Hidden Acres Farm at Green Bluff, pick fresh produce, and then sell it to River Tech families at our Student Farmers Market at 2:15 PM that afternoon.

Student Farmers Market · 2:15 PM

The Student Farmers Market will take place on the lawn to the right of the school entrance. Please do not park on the right side of the school so we can keep that area open for the market.

Please come support our students at 2:15 PM! Bring cash—especially small bills—and bags for your purchases. All proceeds will help cover field-trip expenses. The students will be excited and proud to sell the produce they picked!

We need several parents of full-time students to volunteer as chaperones at Hidden Acres Farm from 10:45 AM to 1:00 PM. If you are available, please text Mary at 425-444-2271.

Hidden Acres Farm

Wednesday, September 16 | 8:30 AM–2:30 PM
Cost: $10 per full-time student

Full-time students

The $10 cost includes:

  • School-bus transportation
  • Teacher supervision
  • Produce picked by the student and sold at the market

Students will begin the day at school at 8:30 AM with Math and English. The bus will leave at approximately 10:00 AM and return in time for the Student Farmers Market.

Participation in this field trip is optional, but there will not be a separate on-campus program for students who choose not to attend. Students may take their Math and English workbooks home on Tuesday to complete their assignments.

Please carefully read the information below. Full-time students must sign up and pay through the form attached below by Sunday, September 13, unless other arrangements have been made with Mary.

Homeschool families

Part-time homeschool families

Hidden Acres Farm at Green Bluff
Wednesday, September 16

Part-time homeschool families should meet us directly at Hidden Acres Farm at 10:45 AM. Please do not drop off part-time students at the school, as River Tech will not provide transportation for them.

Part-time students are welcome to enjoy the farm alongside their full-time River Tech friends at no admission cost.

Please note:

  • A parent or designated adult must provide transportation and remain at the farm to supervise their student.
  • Families must pay for any produce their student picks.
  • River Tech will cover produce costs only for registered full-time students participating in the Student Farmers Market.
  • Part-time students may join a River Tech group when accompanied and supervised by their own adult.

When to finish

Homeschool families attend of their own accord. They are welcome to finish when the full-time students finish, or at a time of their own choosing.

Past field trips & photo gallery

Past Trip Highlights

🧗 Coeur Climbing Company: January 16, 2025

Our River Tech students took on a fun physical challenge: rock climbing! They all had a blast. The gym was buzzing with energy as students tackled routes of varying difficulty, from beginner-friendly walls to advanced climbs that tested their skills and courage.

🥾 Higgens Point Hike: December 4, 2024

Spectacular views of the lake, and we even spotted eagles! Our students learned about Ecclesiastic wisdom: there is a season for everything, a time to be born and a time to die. The eagles and seagulls were having a feast!

🚔 Post Falls Police Department: December 6, 2024

Kids asked the officers so many good questions! They loved learning about what equipment and tools the police use, checking out the evidence room, and climbing into the Rescue 1 vehicle and the police car. A great educational field trip!

🏖️ Quemlin Park: August 28, 2024

Such a fun beach day! Many kids got in the water and swam, some played capture the flag, others dodgeball or volleyball, and some older kids went on a hike.
