RIVER TECH · 2026–27

Dismissal choices

At the 2:30 p.m. dismissal, students either stay with a staff member until collected by a parent or authorized adult, or leave school independently.

Independent dismissal means your child leaves school supervision at dismissal and makes their own way to their ride or follows the arrangements you have made with them.

Elementary students

Elementary students stay with a staff member until a parent or authorized adult collects them. If you would like your child to leave independently, give permission using the elementary form below.

Elementary: independent dismissal opt-in

Middle and high school students

Middle and high school students leave independently at dismissal. If you would like your child to stay with a staff member until a parent or authorized adult collects them, request that using the middle and high school form below.

Middle & high: independent dismissal opt-out

You can change your choice at any time by submitting the same form again.

Use the parent email recorded with the school. Each form accepts more than one child in the same school level. For children in both elementary and middle/high school, complete each form separately.
