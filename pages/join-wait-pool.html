Your next step

Join the free wait pool.

We contact wait-pool families before opening available places to the public. Tell us about your child so we can consider your family when a suitable place opens.

Is River Tech right for your child?

We want your child to enjoy what makes River Tech distinctive. Just as a culinary school suits someone interested in cooking, River Tech suits students interested in technology, performing arts, or both. If neither appeals to your child, another school may be a better fit.

Our culture

River Tech has a distinct culture, and it matters more than anything else on this form. Please take a few minutes to read Our Culture — it is a plain and honest description of who we are.

Tuition, stated plainly

Full-time tuition for 2026–27 is $7,800 for five days or $6,716 for four when paid quarterly. A $250 household enrollment fee for full-time school is due only after a seat is offered and accepted. See the tuition page for all plans, À La Carte fees, and payment options. Eligible Idaho families may apply for the Parental Choice Tax Credit; an award is not automatic.

Anything else?

This one is entirely optional.

Joining is free and does not commit you to enroll or guarantee a place. We contact wait-pool families first; public enrollment happens only if places remain. If your plans change, email learn@rivertech.me.
