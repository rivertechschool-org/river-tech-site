Arrival, Pick-Up & Parking

2026–27 · The Heart, 927 E Polston Ave, Post Falls

Tap the map to open it full size.

Download the one-page guide

One sheet, in colour — or .

Morning drop-off

Doors open 8:10–8:30 a.m. Doors lock at 8:30.

  • Running late? Text your student’s teacher ahead of time.
  • Doors are opened only when requested ahead of time.
  • Please don’t email the school about late drop-off or early pick-up — a text to the teacher is what reaches someone in time.

Afternoon pickup

High school (grades 9–12)2:30 p.m.
Grades 1–82:35 p.m.
Homeschool à la carte2:30 p.m.

Homeschool — à la carte

Arrive 10:25 a.m. on your days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday. Out at 2:30.

How we drive it

  • Grades 1–5: stay in the car line and pull all the way under the awning.
  • Grades 6–12: park in the middle rows and walk your student in.
  • Please never stop in the lane beside the awning — it has to stay clear.

Good to know

  • The front door is the only exit for every student.
  • Anyone not on your pickup list will be asked for photo ID.
  • Same-day changes: text your student’s teacher as early as you can.

Everyone enters from Mullan Ave and exits onto Polston Ave.

Questions? Email learn@rivertech.me.
