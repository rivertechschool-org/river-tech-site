Discovering their purpose.

FROM INTEREST TO ENROLLMENT

Here’s what happens next.

The same simple process applies to both programs.

1

Tell us about your child.

Join the free wait pool. The form takes about five minutes and asks about your family, your child’s interests, the program you’re considering, and when you hope to start. You can choose either program or say you’re open to both.

No payment. No commitment.

2

We look for the right opening.

We contact wait-pool families before offering unfilled places through public enrollment. Openings depend on the grade, program, and attendance days available. Joining does not guarantee a place or a start date.

While you wait, you’re welcome at River Tech Days, concerts, and productions. If your plans change, let us know.

3

Decide when a seat is offered.

We contact you with an available place and give you a few days to decide. The one-time household enrollment fee is due only when you accept. Accepted families receive the information they need to prepare for their first day.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things
worth knowing.

We want you to understand both the opportunity and the commitment.

Full-time or part-time?

We offer both. Full-time school serves grades 1–12, with four or five days a week of core academics, performing arts, and technology. Our part-time À La Carte program lets homeschool students ages 9–15 join us for one to four days a week.

You can indicate either program, or both, on the wait-pool form.

Explore full-time school →
Explore À La Carte →
What does it cost?

Joining the wait pool is free. Tuition depends on your program, attendance days, and payment plan. Review the current rates and household enrollment fee before joining so you know what to expect if a place becomes available.

Compare tuition and payment plans →
What if we need financial assistance?

The wait-pool form asks whether enrollment would depend on a River Tech scholarship. Tell us there so we understand your situation. Joining does not guarantee financial assistance or a place.

Read about financial assistance →
Can a child start during the school year?

Yes, when an appropriate seat is available. Tell us your preferred timing on the wait-pool form, including whether you can be flexible.

What should we know about the school’s culture?

River Tech is a Christian school. Prayer, worship, and biblical teaching are part of school life, and every program reflects a Christian worldview. Before joining the wait pool, read our culture page and consider whether it describes the community you want for your family.
