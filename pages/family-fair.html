A free service for our school community

Family Fair

A free directory where River Tech parents, children, and staff can introduce themselves and share what they do with one another.

River Tech provides Family Fair as a service to our families and staff. We charge no listing fees, receive no commissions, and earn no income from the directory or any arrangements people make.

Everyone may browse. Verified River Tech parents and staff may create listings. A parent must manage each student listing.

My listings

New listings and edits go to the school for approval before appearing here publicly.

Family Fair helps our families and staff get to know one another. Participants communicate and make any arrangements directly with each other. River Tech reviews listings before they appear and does not handle sales or payments.

Student listings are managed by a parent. Please keep student last names, ages, schedules, and home addresses off listings and photos.
