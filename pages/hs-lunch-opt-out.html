2026–27 SCHOOL YEAR

High School Off-Campus Lunch

Parent opt-out form

High school students may leave campus during lunch rotation unless their parent or guardian opts them out.

We recommend allowing your student to participate. If you agree, no form is needed.

How lunch works

11:45 a.m.–12:10 p.m.
Teacher-directed P.E. activity. Students must complete this before leaving.
From 12:10 p.m.
Students may leave campus during the regular lunch rotation.
By 1:00 p.m.
Students must be back on campus and ready for their next class, which begins at 1:00 p.m.
Special activities
On days with a field trip, school party, or another activity requiring their participation, students must follow the activity schedule and may not leave for off-campus lunch.
A school privilege
Off-campus lunch is a privilege. River Tech may suspend or revoke this privilege for an individual student or the entire class, temporarily or permanently.
