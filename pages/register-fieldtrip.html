Where & when

Location: River City Lanes Bowling
Date: Wednesday, November 4, 2026
Bowling time: 10:00–11:30 AM
Cost: $12 per person, including bowling, shoe rental, and taxes.

Full-time students

Drop off at school between 8:10–8:30 AM and pick up at 2:30 PM. Transportation will be provided by our school bus, although a few Junior High and High School students may ride with Jordan, Caitlin, Luke, or Mary.

Part-time students

Meet us at River City Lanes at 9:45 AM and pick up by 11:30 AM. You are welcome to join us for park play after; see the school schedule below.

Part-time students must have a supervising adult. For this specific field trip, part-time students ages 13 and older may be dropped off with a River Tech teacher, without their own supervising adult. For students ages 12 and younger, text Mary to request permission to drop off your child without a supervising adult, if needed and if your child is independent enough.

Activities & cost

  • Bowling: $12 per participant, including shoe rental and taxes. Payment is collected through the sign-up form. River City Lanes will have a cosmic light show and song requests!
  • Arcade games: Optional! Students may bring extra cash to play arcade games. Not included in the bowling fee.
  • Food & snacks: The full River City Lanes menu will be available for purchase. Students may also bring their own cold lunch, snacks, or small treats to share with classmates and friends.
  • Costumes: Students are welcome to dress up! Please avoid costumes that may frighten younger children. No face-covering masks, weapons, blood, or gore.

Part-time student chaperones: If you would like to bowl, please sign up through the form and pay the $12 fee. If you are only coming to supervise, there is no need to sign up or pay. Just show up with your registered student.

School schedule for November 4

There will be no mandatory regular school classes on November 4. If your full-time student is not signed up and paid for the field trip, they will need to stay home that day.

For participating full-time students, we will begin with our usual morning assembly and have Math and English classes before and after the field trip. Weather permitting, we also plan to visit the Real Life outdoor and indoor playgrounds from 11:45 AM–12:45 PM before returning to school. Part-time students are welcome to join us at the playgrounds as well if they would like, with their supervising adult!

Please note that the schedule and playground destination are subject to change.

If you need to drop off your full-time student late or pick them up early, please text their Math or English teacher directly to coordinate. If you are unsure whom to contact, text Mary.

Sign-up & payment

Deadline: Sunday, November 1, at midnight (Pacific time).

Complete the form and payment to reserve your student's spot. If you miss the deadline, text Mary to check whether space is still available. Spots cannot be guaranteed after Sunday. Late places require Mary's approval and payment to Mary in cash.
