MONDAY · PERFORMING ARTS

Find their voice. Share the stage.

Music, theater, voice, and movement give students a place to express themselves and create something together. Monday brings them into the rehearsal room, where individual effort becomes a shared performance.

A place for every voice

Performing arts are central to life at River Tech. Students explore singing, acting, choir, and stagecraft with a team of six teachers and production leaders. Confident performers have room to stretch; students finding their voice receive encouragement and practice. Both learn to listen, communicate, and contribute.

Real productions. Shared accomplishment.

Students prepare full musical theater productions and community concerts throughout the year. They learn what it takes to carry a performance from rehearsal to opening night: knowing their part, responding to direction, and supporting the ensemble. Costumes and props help bring the story to life, with each member of the team contributing to the finished production.

TUESDAY · SCIENCE & SOCIAL STUDIES

Understand the world. Learn to live wisely.

Science helps students understand the natural world. Social studies explores people, places, and civilizations. Tuesday brings these subjects together with practical lessons in money, relationships, and leadership.

Understand the world around them

Students investigate, experiment, and ask questions in science. Big Picture Geography connects places with creation, history, and human civilization. With an approximately 1:12 student-to-teacher ratio and a competency-based approach, teachers can give students personal attention as they build understanding and master concepts.

Prepare for everyday life

Financial literacy and social development are taught with each age group in mind. Younger students learn about money and how to make and keep friends. Older students study personal finance, family life, and leadership. These classes connect schoolwork with decisions and responsibilities students will encounter beyond the classroom.

THURSDAY · LIFE SKILLS

Think clearly. Create with confidence.

Clear thinking, thoughtful writing, and creative work belong together. Thursday gives students time to develop their ideas, express them well, and consider the convictions that guide their lives.

Skills that shape a person

In writing and debate, students practice explaining an idea, examining the reasons behind it, and listening carefully to others. Fine art and crafts give them another way to explore and express what they see. In a small school where teachers know students and their families, these lessons become opportunities for personal guidance as well as skill development.

Faith, character, and direction

Our Christian faith connects learning with character and purpose. Students are encouraged to consider how their choices affect others and how their abilities can be used well. We want them to grow in wisdom, confidence, and a sense of responsibility to their community.

FRIDAY · TECHNOLOGY

Turn curiosity into something that works.

On Fridays, students turn ideas into working projects through coding, robotics, filmmaking, and digital arts. Beginners and experienced tinkerers alike can develop the skills to create with technology.

Create with technology

Students write code, build and program robots, and explore digital tools, including AI. Projects connect technical skills with a purpose: what are we trying to make, who will use it, and how can we improve it? The work gives students practice in testing ideas, solving problems, and learning from mistakes.

Build skill. Develop judgment.

Technical confidence matters most when it is paired with good judgment. Students learn to understand the tools they use, question their limitations, and choose them thoughtfully. The aim is to help students create useful things and make informed decisions about technology.
