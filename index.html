A school where students want to show up.

River Tech is a school of performing arts and technology in Post Falls, Idaho. Our teachers, curriculum, and competency-based learning model help students build confidence, develop their gifts, and master what they learn.

CHRISTIAN. FAMILY-CENTERED. LEADERSHIP-BUILDING.

Faith shapes how we learn and how we live.

River Tech is a Christian school built on biblical principles. Prayer, worship, and gratitude are part of every day. We raise kids to love God, honor their families, contribute to their community, take responsibility for themselves, work hard, and build things worth building.
